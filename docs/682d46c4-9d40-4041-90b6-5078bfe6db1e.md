Some things require "situations" to be effective. Lets say you hear a song and it doesn't affect you, but it affects your friend. They ball out crying. It spoke to them in some way. Now, lets say your at a disco. You like the idea of dancing, but are afraid to do it. You can be motivated into it, by a friend, by letting go, etc. Now that can happen with the song too. You just have to find the "frame" where it makes sense. Cathedral of my own cage works when you feel held back by yourself even though you want to move forward. If you didn't get any emotional generation from it the first time, try thinking about times where this was true for you as you listen to it. This kind of neuroplasticity is a skill. For you it might only be minorly true, or it is something you have already mastered to the point where it does not affect you.
