I see someone in pain. Someone I care about. I feel their pain. I get a strong compulsion to work out their pain for them. And that starts with fixing it in myself. That means I have to go through the pain that may not even be mine (though it usually is, just something im not focused on and they bring it to focus by being in it). The compulsion can be so strong that failure or setbacks can cause my own mental instability. Progress calms me.

People see me with negative feelings from this, and assume that its me doing bad, when actually living my life to the fullest and how I desire, drowned in the pain of others. My "Processing" can get mistaken as my own pain, which it partly is. They will feel negative feelings and then shove it onto me as if that would help in any way. So I have to deal with what I'm outputting to them, as well as what they are inputting to me. When this gets negative, the entire space is negative and there's no way to push through other than more processing, which to them would look like I'm failing even though I'm succeeding. I think it would be interesting to interact with a person that also has this strong compulsion or even desire, and generate some sort of feedback loop.
