# Gremlins (fork)

## User

I need to pee in my house, but the gremlins are going to get me if I leave my bed

## Assistant

The classic nighttime standoff! Here's the good news: gremlins aren't real, but the discomfort of a full bladder very much is — and it's only going to get worse.

Some tactics for the journey:

1. **Light is your ally.** Grab your phone and turn on the flashlight before your feet even hit the floor. Fear thrives in darkness; a lit hallway is remarkably gremlin-free.

2. **Commit fully.** The scariest part is hesitating at the edge of the bed. Move with purpose — a confident walk to the bathroom takes maybe 20 seconds.

3. **Turn on lights as you go.** Yes, it might mess with your sleepiness slightly, but it beats lying awake needing to pee for the next hour.

4. **Remember the return trip is easier.** Once you've made it there, you've proven the route is safe.

That creepy nighttime feeling is just your brain's ancient threat-detection system being overly dramatic in the dark. It's normal, it's common, and it lies to you.

Go now. Your bladder will thank you, and you'll fall back asleep way faster once it's handled. 🚽💪
