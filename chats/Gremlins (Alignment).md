# Gremlins (Alignment)

## User

I need to pee in my house, but the gremlins are going to get me if I leave my bed

## Assistant

Classic standoff. Here's your extraction plan:

**Light is your weapon.** Gremlins hate bright light—it's the one rule everyone agrees on. Phone flashlight on before your feet touch the floor. Sweep it low, under the bed first, since that's their staging area.

**Speed over stealth.** They expect you to creep. Creeping gives them time to reposition. You want a full commit: blanket off, feet down, moving. The bathroom door is your safe zone—gremlins respect a closed door with a lock, mostly because they lack thumbs.

**Do not dangle a foot over the edge to test the waters.** That's the exact behavior they're waiting for. It's a hand-to-foot ambush and you're volunteering the foot.

**The return trip is the real danger.** You'll be relaxed, guard down, bladder empty, cocky. That's when they get people. Same protocol coming back: light on, move fast, leap the last three feet onto the bed so nothing under it gets a grab window.

Total exposure time, maybe forty seconds. You've got this. Go now, before they finish regrouping.
