## Assistant

# PRESENCE
### A working definition and its complications

---

## The Core Idea

Presence is when mind and reality are communicating.

Not mind *observing* reality. Not mind *narrating* reality. Communicating — a two-way channel. Reality sends; you receive without translating it into something else first. You send back attention, and reality responds by getting sharper.

When the channel is open, you're present. When the mind starts talking to itself *about* reality instead of *with* it, the channel closes. That's the whole mechanism.

---

## What Breaks the Channel

- **Rehearsal** — running future conversations instead of the current one
- **Replay** — editing the past instead of receiving the present
- **Commentary** — the inner narrator describing the moment to you, which is not the same as being in it
- **Craving an outcome** — you stop listening to what *is* because you're scanning for what you *want*

---

## The Emotional Fine Print

This is where most definitions cheat, so let's not.

**Presence is not calm.** The channel doesn't filter. If reality is sending grief, presence means receiving grief at full signal. People avoid presence precisely because it works — distraction is a survival tool, and sometimes a legitimate one.

**Presence can hurt more before it hurts less.** Emotions you've been narrating around (safe, contained, third-person) become first-person again. That's the cost of the open channel.

**But un-received emotion doesn't leave.** It waits. Communication is how it moves through. The dodge is rent, not purchase.

**Presence with another person is double exposure.** Their reality is now on your channel too. This is why real listening is rare — it's not a skill shortage, it's a willingness shortage.

---

## Quick Diagnostics

| Signal | Channel status |
|---|---|
| Time feels textured, not blurred | Open |
| You could describe the last five minutes | Open |
| You "came to" mid-task with no memory of it | Closed |
| You're composing your reply while they're talking | Closed |

## One-Line Version

Presence isn't peace. It's honesty at full bandwidth — and reality only talks to people who pick up.
