## Assistant

eee is doing one of three jobs, and they're all vibes rather than questions:

1. **The squeal** — excitement leaking out sideways. Something good happened, or is about to, and words were too slow.
2. **The poke** — "is this thing on?" Minimal effort, maximal deniability. If nobody answers, you never really said anything.
3. **The meme** — descendant of the great "E" of 2018, when a single letter became the funniest possible object for reasons nobody could articulate, which was the joke.

The space around it in the data is almost entirely joy or absurdity — nobody types "eee" while filing taxes. It sits next to keysmashes, "!!!", and that specific register of internet where enthusiasm outruns grammar.

Most likely branch: you're poking. Consider yourself poked back.
