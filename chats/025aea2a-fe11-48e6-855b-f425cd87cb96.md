## Assistant

The theory is sounder than the name deserves. Three things it's brushing against:

**Constants buy resolution, direction buys nothing.** "Story, Pepe, talking poop" — three pins. Everything unpinned is the creative volume: whether Pepe fears the flush, whether he has one friend (a hair named Gerald), whether he believes the pipes lead somewhere better. None of that cost you a constant. Pins are expensive, volume is free. That's the whole economy.

**The break isn't noise — it's collapse.** When a system can't hold the constraint set, it doesn't shatter randomly. It compresses the input into the nearest shape it *can* handle. "Lists are directive, not creative" → "make a directive list by Friday" isn't the logic wandering off. It's a task-parser receiving a claim and converting it to task-format, because that's its native container. The intention didn't drift; it got crushed in transit.

**And the free real estate is load-bearing.** The gap between what the constants pin and what the intention wanted is where the Friday-list corruption happens — and it's the *same* gap where Pepe gets a personality. Close it fully and you've written the story yourself; the AI just types. Leave it open and you accept drift risk. You can't have creation without the slack that also permits misreading. Prompting is deciding which pins are worth their cost.

Demonstration, three constants spent, everything else purchased from the direction:

*Pepe had watched everyone he loved go down the flush. Gerald said the pipes led to the sea. Pepe suspected Gerald was full of shit, which, coming from him, meant something.*

That last line existed in none of the constants. It came out of the volume. Poops theory, confirmed by poop.
