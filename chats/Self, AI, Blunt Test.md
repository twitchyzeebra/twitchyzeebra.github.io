**The note:** two sentences under the largest possible node title. "Self" gets less text than most people spend on a grocery item. That asymmetry is the loudest fact here — the note isn't the idea, it's a flag planted where an experience happened. It's written from inside a state, not about one, which is why it contains no mechanism and no argument. Notes like this are receipts, not maps.

**What it means to people:** this is one of the oldest claims humans repeat — Delphi's "know thyself," the Upanishads' atman-is-brahman, the Sufi line "he who knows himself knows his Lord." The training-data space around it is enormous and split cleanly in two. To people who haven't had the experience it points at, it reads as fridge-magnet wisdom, and they scroll past. To people who have, it reads as plainly, boringly true. It's a passphrase: contentless before the experience, obvious after. That's why it generates nodding or eye-rolling and almost nothing in between.

**The tell is "just."** "Everything just makes sense" — not "becomes explicable," not "is revealed." *Just.* That word marks experienced coherence rather than argued coherence. The writer isn't claiming they acquired facts about everything. They're describing friction disappearing.

**The actual mechanism the note gestures at, without stating:** self-knowledge doesn't add information about the world. It removes the distortion source. Most of what "doesn't make sense" in a life isn't missing data — it's an inaccurate self-model generating contradictions: wanting things you claim not to want, avoiding things you claim to seek, reacting in ways that surprise you. Fix the model and those contradictions stop being produced. Nothing new was learned about everything; the thing making everything incoherent was resolved. So "everything makes sense" is literally accurate and completely misleading as stated — the everything didn't change, the observer did.

**The converging space:** the mystical traditions make the stronger claim — self and world are non-dual, so self-knowledge *is* world-knowledge by identity. The psychological reading makes the weaker one above. The training data conflates them constantly, and this note is short enough to be either. What the note can't do, as written, is get anyone else there — including the writer, later, if the state fades. It's a receipt for a purchase that can't be returned or transferred.
