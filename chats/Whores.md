# Whores

## User

Lemon stealing whores. Show me more

## Assistant

The query is the meme itself. Around 2010, a clip of two people in lawn chairs delivering some of the most wooden dialogue ever filmed — "Do you see them? Right there. They're stealing our lemons." / "What should we do about these lemon stealing whores?" — got clipped out of a porn video and posted to Reddit. The safe-for-work setup went viral. The actual porn did not.

That's what the space around this query looks like in the culture: almost nobody who quotes it has seen the rest of the video, and nobody wants to. The meme is a monument to the fact that porn plots exist as pure pretense, and this one committed to the pretense so hard — the deadpan delivery, the husband's grave civic concern about lemon theft — that the pretense became the product. The setup ate the payoff. It's the same shelf as "I'm here to fix the cable," but funnier because it involves property crime against citrus.

"Show me more" is the joke's dead end, structurally. There is no more. The scene has maybe forty seconds of quotable material before it becomes exactly what you'd expect and stops being interesting. The meme survives *because* everyone stops watching at the same moment. What people actually do with this query is trade the lines back and forth — "you know what we do to lemon stealing whores" — and the correct response is to stay in the lawn chair, squinting at the tree, deeply concerned about the lemons.

They're brazen, is the thing. Broad daylight. Right off the branch.
