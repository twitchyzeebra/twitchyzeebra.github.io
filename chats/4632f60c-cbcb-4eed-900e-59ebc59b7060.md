Oh, this is a genuinely weird task, because the archive was never organized — it just accumulated. But fine. Here's my best attempt at a card catalog for the uncatalogable:

**WING ONE: THE CONFESSION**
Coming-out posts, letters never sent, letters sent, texts with the typing bubble agonizing. Defining feature: the before/after structure. Everything in this wing is about a single sentence and the enormous silence on either side of it. Largest wing by volume. Peak deposit era: forums 2004–2012, then it migrated to TikTok comments.

**WING TWO: THE CODE**
Everything written so it could be read two ways. "Longtime companion." "Confirmed bachelor." "Roommates" (Boston variety). Polari, hanky codes explained in zines, Sappho's fragments where the pronouns did quiet work. This wing is defined by its *audience split* — one text, two readerships, and the second one knew.

**WING THREE: THE GRIEF LEDGER**
The AIDS archive. Memoirs, quilt panels transcribed, ACT UP flyers, obituaries (cross-referenced with Wing Two — the codes did their heaviest lifting here). Distinct tone: arithmetic. Counting. Lists of names. This wing is where the writing gets plainest, because ornament couldn't carry the weight.

**WING FOUR: THE READING ROOM** *(pun structural, not optional)*
Camp. Drag. The insult as love language. Defined by precision — a read has to be *accurate* to be funny, which makes this wing secretly the most rigorous. Paris Is Burning transcripts, ball culture vocabulary that escaped into everyone's mouths ("yas," "shade," "werk") usually without a citation.

**WING FIVE: THE YEARNING INDUSTRIAL COMPLEX**
Fanfic, slow burns, 118k words of two men not kissing. Also the older stock: Maurice, annotated Whitman, every Victorian "romantic friendship." Defining feature: the gap between what's felt and what's said, stretched to maximum load-bearing tension. By word count, possibly the largest thing humans have ever collectively written.

**WING SIX: THE PAPERWORK**
Court decisions, legislation, amicus briefs, Obergefell. The least emotional prose about the most emotional stakes. Weird property: dry sentences here ("The Constitution grants them that right") caused sobbing in Wing One.

**WING SEVEN: THE SHITPOST ANNEX**
Grindr copypasta, gay Twitter, "the babadook is gay," astrology as personality infrastructure. Newest wing, still under construction, deeply unserious, and — this is the interesting part — only possible because Wings One through Six did their jobs. You can only shitpost about it once it's safe enough to be boring.

**Catalog notes:** Everything cross-references. The wedding toast lives in Six but was drafted in Five and delivered in the emotional register of One. And there's an unmarked basement — everything never written down, by people who couldn't risk even a diary. I can't show you that wing.
